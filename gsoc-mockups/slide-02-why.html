The Problem
Why Unified Logging Is Needed in RUXAILAB
What RUXAILAB Already Has
Answer Collection
Task answers, SUS scores, NASA-TLX, and questionnaire responses are stored per participant.
Task Timing
Elapsed time per task is recorded automatically when participants complete or skip tasks.
Moderated Video Calls
Real-time WebRTC sessions with facilitator, participant, and observator roles.
Analytics & Reports
Basic statistics and visualizations of aggregated participant answers.
What's Missing
Event-Level Logging
No structured record of individual actions, system events, or API calls during a study session.
Cross-Layer Traceability
Cannot correlate a technical event (frame drop) with a methodological event (observer note) or AI output.
AI Decision Audit Trail
When AI models make decisions (gaze detection, sentiment), there is no log of why or how confident.
Reproducible Export
No way to export a full, anonymized session log for IRB, peer review, or secondary analysis.
