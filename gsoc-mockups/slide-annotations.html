Show less at first glance

Filters, AI traces, and advanced options are tucked away by default. Researchers see what they need — not a wall of controls — the moment they land on the page.

2

Problems float to the top

KPI cards are color-coded so protocol deviations and anomalies catch your eye immediately, without hunting through logs.

Built to slot into existing code

The table layout reuses Vuetify's v-data-table, meaning the dev implementing this won't need to write custom grid logic from scratch.
